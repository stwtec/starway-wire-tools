Frequently asked questions

Why is no complete M16878 number built for /11–/13?

The catalog labels these pages “equivalent wire.” Their dimensions and Thermax numbers refer to tin-plated commercial products. Confirm availability of a silver-plated MIL-spec version with the supplier; do not treat the commercial rows as MIL part numbers.

What does (REF) mean?

The catalog uses (REF) for alternate constructions that conform to the specification but are not specifically included in MIL-W-16878. Their dimensions can be viewed without inventing a MIL part number.
