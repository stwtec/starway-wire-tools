Starway Technology specializes in high-reliability wire harness and cable assembly manufacturing for aerospace, defense, and industrial applications, with custom manufacturing and quality verification services.

M22759 (SAE AS22759) wires are high-performance, medium and high-temperature hookup wires with extruded PTFE or ETFE, or PTFE/polyimide tape insulators, and stranded conductors.

These wires are designed for severe-environment aerospace applications, such as engine controls and SWAMP areas. Some types are available with highly abrasion-resistant insulation and / or high-strength conductors for maximum performance under mechanical stresses.

FAQ

1. What is an M22759 Slant Sheet?

The Slant Sheet is the specification series designator. In M22759/6-20-7, for example, /6 identifies the applicable construction, including insulation, conductor plating, temperature rating, and voltage rating.

2. How does M22759 color coding work?

An M22759 color code may contain up to four digits. The first digit identifies the base color, and each following digit identifies a spiral stripe color. For example, M22759/8-9045 is a white wire with black, yellow, and green spiral stripes. Color codes are 0 Black, 1 Brown, 2 Red, 3 Orange, 4 Yellow, 5 Green, 6 Blue, 7 Violet, 8 Gray, and 9 White. For /83 through /85, color 5D dark green is preferred, and color 9 white is an acceptable alternate.

3. Why does the lookup return no matching part number?

A part number is valid only when its Slant Sheet, AWG size, and color code combination is permitted by the specification. Available AWG sizes vary by Slant Sheet, and /83 through /85 accept only the specified color codes 5D or 9.
